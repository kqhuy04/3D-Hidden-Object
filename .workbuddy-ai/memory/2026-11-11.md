# 2026-11-11 — nine bug reports: view, run, crawl, stairs, tower, arms, FPS

All nine items from the user's message are now closed. Five real bugs, four of
them in the collision resolver.

**1. The crawl could not pass under anything.** `triangleIsSolid` gated a
triangle on its *lowest corner* (`tb[o+2] <= headY`). A diagonal brace is one
long triangle, so the watchtower's bracing — whose surface is 1.2 m over a
crawler's head at the point the body meets it — counted as a wall for its whole
length. The body stopped dead 30 cm short of the tower's south face, standing or
crawling, at every capsule height from 0.5 to 1.75.

Fixed in the narrow phase: after `circleTriangle2D` succeeds, `surfaceYAt`
interpolates the triangle's height at the contact point and skips the contact if
that height is above `headY`. It returns `-Infinity` when the triangle's XZ
projection is degenerate, i.e. the triangle is vertical — a wall panel projects
to a line and every point along it is a different height of the same wall, so
those keep the bounds test. Measured before/after on the tower's south face:
crawl 0.75 m went from blocked at z −27.90 to reaching z −30.02 (the interior).

**2. The last step of a staircase was refused.** The step allowance lived on the
part being *stepped onto*. The kits' top tread meets a deck belonging to another
prop, that prop has no bonus, and a 0.5 m last step was rejected where the four
before it were allowed. Added `stepBonusUnder()` — the allowance is read from the
ground underfoot — and `supportHeight` / `collectContacts` / `deepestContact` now
use `max(part.stepBonus, carry)`. Guarded by `bonusParts`, so maps with no stairs
pay one comparison.

**3. A 0.34 m first tread left the body stuck on step one.** `triangleIsSolid`
measured "low enough to step over" from the prop's base until the feet cleared
`base + stepHeight` — deliberately, to stop a low rail becoming a step on a
slope. The arcology plaza stair's risers are 0.34 m, one centimetre under the
limit, so on tread 1 the feet never cleared it and every riser above stayed
measured from the ground. On a flight (`bonus > 0`) the reference is now always
the feet. Plaza stair: was stuck at 0.34 m, now walks 11.5 → 21.2 in x and
0 → 4.01 m up.

**4. Three of four commuters stayed in a T-pose.** `ARM_BONE` was
`/^(Left|Right)(Upper)?Arm$/`; the exporter suffixes each copy's bones
(`LeftArm_3`, `_4`, `_6`), so exactly one of the four was fixed. Widened to
allow `(_\d+)?`. Verified: all four now at y ≈ −0.96.

**5. The interactable selector cost 1.5 ms/frame on `office` (891 props).** One
`raycastSegment` through that map is ~0.26-0.74 ms, and the selector spent up to
ten per frame. Added a per-candidate sight cache (`SIGHT_TTL 200 ms`,
`SIGHT_MOVE 0.5 m`, cleared on `setProps`/`resetRound`/`reset`, bounded at 256
entries). 1.53 → 0.43 ms.

**The watchtower is deliberately not climbable.** The rung detector that was
added for it produced thirteen "ladders" out of one tower — its bracing reads as
a column of small horizontal faces a third of a metre apart, and it repeats
exactly every 4 m. The only real ladder is a red-and-white one between two
platforms that cannot be reached from the ground. `findRungColumns` is deleted;
`findClimbVolumes` is back to named `<map>-ladder` props only, and
`tryEnterClimb` now requires the feet within 1.2 m of the volume's bottom so a
compound prop's railing cannot be grabbed from the ground.

**Verified:** 56 tests, `tsc` clean, build 754 kB; 15/15 objects pickable on the
three maps; V toggles third/first (body hidden in first); Shift is a run gear and
plays `Run`; C holds `Crawl` on frame 1 at rest (`paused`, `time 0`) and plays it
when moving at 1.82 m/s.

**Harness lesson (third time).** `h.game.loop.stop()` stops rendering, so a
screenshot taken afterwards shows the *previous* frame — the tower probe kept
photographing the spawn. And `startNewRound()` must be awaited: a 400 ms sleep
lets it finish after `setPosition`, teleporting the body back. Both produced
confident, wrong readings.

## Round 2 — UI redesign + movement comfort

The user asked for a full interface redesign ("chỉn chu, hoàn thiện") with an
explicit ban on gradients ("dễ nhận đây là game AI make"), plus a human
play-test. Answers to my questions: any style I like, keep the UI in English,
keep the 3-strikes / 5-minute-hint rules exactly as they are.

**Design system — "printed field guide".** Cream paper (`#f4ecd9`), warm black
ink (`#191510`), one rust accent (`#b0472a`). Serif display (Iowan/Palatino/
Georgia) over uppercase micro-labels, mono for numbers and key caps. Depth is a
hard offset shadow at low opacity, never a blur; press feedback collapses the
shadow and moves the button into the page. **Zero gradients in the file** — that
is now a stated rule, not an accident.

Rewritten: `src/app/styles.css` (whole file), `index.html` loading screen, and
the markup of `MainMenu`, `GameHUD`, `PauseMenu`, `ResultsScreen`, `MapSelect`.
Every emoji is gone (🔍 🗺 ⏱️ ⏸ 🏆 📷 ▶ ↺ ⌂). The five slots are numbered
(`01`…`05`) with a hairline between rows; the tick is always in the DOM and only
becomes visible when a row is found, so the row never changes width. The reticle
is 18px, dark line over a white core, and turns rust + scales up when something
is in reach — that is the "you are on target" signal. The map picker now shows
each map's own brief instead of "1,073 objects · 3.8 MB", which was build
metadata and made every card look like a debug list.

**Real bugs found by looking at the render.**
- `.controls-modal` had no `.hidden` rule and there is no generic `.hidden` in
  the stylesheet, so "How to play" was open on the main menu at all times. It is
  now a proper modal with a backdrop-click close.
- `.btn-secondary` and `.btn-outline` were two creams 8% apart — no hierarchy.
  Now rust / ink / no-fill, three unmistakable levels.
- The pickup prompt sat at `50% + 40px`, i.e. on the character's knees. Moved to
  `+74px`, below the body.
- `showFeedback` used to add `animate-bounce`, whose keyframes start at
  `opacity: 0`. On a frame-starved machine the banner is invisible until the
  animation clock ticks — precisely when "2 MISTAKES LEFT" matters. Animation
  removed; the banner appears fully opaque.

**Movement comfort.** `walkSpeed` was 4.8 m/s (17 km/h — faster than a
race-walker) against a Walk clip authored for 3.0, so the clip played at 1.6x
and the feet skated. Now 3.2 m/s: Walk at 1.07x, Run at 0.8x (5.6 m/s), Crawl at
0.81x (1.22 m/s). Measured with `tmp-gears.mjs`, which drives `Game.update` at a
fixed 60 Hz.

**The "crawl does not move" bug was not a bug.** `GameLoop` clamps delta to
0.1 s, and SwiftShader renders at 1.5 fps, so 400 ms of real time is often zero
frames — every "it did not react" reading was "it was never asked". Driven
through `Game.update` at 60 Hz, C-then-W moves 1.82 m/s (now 1.22), the clip
unpauses, and releasing W re-pauses it on frame 1. `tmp-crawl3.mjs` is the
harness that settles this class of question; never judge input from the live
loop under SwiftShader.

**Verification.** 56/56 tests, `tsc --noEmit` clean, `npm run build` 755 kB,
`tmp-uiflow.mjs` 19/19 (menu → hunt → first person → pause → change map → back).

## Round 3 — the other fourteen maps

Walked `construction-site`, `countryside-railway`, `cozy-farm`, `dia-de-muertos`,
`frontier-town`, `japanese-suburban-city-street`, `medieval-village`,
`modern-restaurant-cafe`, `neighborhood-pathways`, `pirate-cove`, `skatepark`,
`space-base`, `stadium`, `symphony-stagecraft` — spawn view, eight walk
directions, camera occlusion, reachable area, object distance, and a screenshot
of the first second of play.

**Two real bugs, both fixed.**

1. **`dia-de-muertos` spawned the player below its own town.** The sidecar hint
   is `(2.51, 3.5, 3.91)` — the square is at 3.19 — but the ring search took the
   first standable cell at `r = 1.5`, which was 2.8 m lower on the slope outside
   the platform. Result: a 3.4 m boom (against 8.6 m everywhere else), the camera
   in the sand, and the town visible only as a sliver. `findSpawn` now requires
   the candidate floor to be within `LEVEL_TOLERANCE` (1.2 m) of the hint's own
   height, with an `Infinity` fallback for a bad sidecar. The body now lands at
   `(5.28, 3.19, 2.76)`, the boom is 8.6 m, and the walkable area went from 2567
   to 4983 cells.

2. **T-posed characters on fourteen maps.** `relaxPosedArms` only matched
   `commuter|pedestrian|citizen|mannequin|bystander|passer`, so the pirate
   captain, the astronaut, the pilot, the commander, the police officer, the
   construction workers and the stadium players all stood with both arms straight
   out. Rewritten to work from the rig: one pass to collect unique skeletons, then
   drop the arms only when **both** are horizontal. A rig with one arm out is
   posed on purpose and is left alone — that is why the stadium's fielders (1/2
   arms) still hold their pose while its T-posed `gridiron-player` was fixed.
   `pirate-cove` 17 → 0, `space-base` 21 → 0, `construction-site` 17 → 0.

**Checked and clean.** 105/105 objects rest on a surface (the one apparent
floater, `hearth-duck`, is sitting exactly on `boulder_1`, which is scenery and
not in the collision set). 5/5 pickable on six more maps. No wall between camera
and body on any map. All 21 spawns resolve on their own level with 8/8 free walk
directions after the change.

**Design note, not fixed:** 18 of 105 objects sit further than 20 m from spawn —
`chronos-hearth` 40.8 m, `chrome-arcology-transit` 39.3 m,
`japanese-suburban-city-street` 31.2 m, and the rest between 20.5 and 26 m. The
20 m figure was my operationalisation of "do not send the player wandering"; on
those maps the objects are spread over 22-42 m rather than clustered. Moving them
means rewriting their hint text too, so it is a content decision for the user, not
a bug fix.

**Harness corrections.** `tmp-playtest.mjs` moves only the camera and then presses
E — the body is still ten metres away, so the 3.2 m reach gate always fails and it
reports "prompt shown, nothing picked up". It is not a pickup gate; `tmp-pickable`
is. `tmp-itemcheck` called the private `supportHeight` with a step height instead
of a capsule, which made every answer `NaN` and reported 35 broken objects that
were fine. Both written into MEMORY.md.

## Performance pass + production shell

The user played and reported low FPS, and asked for whatever a production game
has that this one lacks. Both done.

### Where the frame time actually went

Profile on `chrome-arcology-transit` (SwiftShader, 1258x646): 1065 meshes, 469
draw calls, 283k scene triangles, **1063 shadow casters**, a 2048² shadow map
over a ±45 m box, rebuilt **every frame**; and every frame drawn through an
`EffectComposer` with a 4×MSAA target. Shadow on 53.7 ms, off 14.7 ms — 73% of
the frame was the shadow pass.

### What changed

- **`src/game/render/SunShadows.ts`** (new) owns the hemisphere + directional
  light. `renderer.shadowMap.autoUpdate = false`; the map is rebuilt at most
  every `max(presetRefreshMs, frameMs * 2.5)` and never while paused or in a
  menu. The box shrank to ±30 m at 1024² (4.4× fewer texels) and now **follows
  the player** on a 6 m / 4 m grid instead of being pinned to the origin — the
  old rig left a player 60 m from spawn with no shadow at all. `castShadow` is
  only set above a 0.18 m bounding radius (1063 → 974 casters).
- **`src/game/render/Quality.ts`** now drives `setPixelRatio` through the preset
  (`min(dpr, cap) * adaptiveScale`) instead of `min(dpr, 2.0)`, and gained
  `shadowRadius` / `shadowType` / `shadowRefreshMs` / `msaa` per preset.
- **`OutlineHighlight`** is entered only while something is selected, and its
  buffer is `HalfFloatType`. Rendering into a target skips the renderer's tone
  mapping, so the old 8-bit buffer clamped HDR before ACES — invisible while the
  composer was the only path, a 1.4% brightness pop once the paths alternate.
  Verified by reading framebuffer pixels (`tmp-parity.mjs`), not by eye.
- **Lights moved out of the loaded map** into `Game`'s scene, so a map swap no
  longer disposes an 8 MB shadow map.
- `GameHUD.updateTimer` no longer writes `textContent` sixty times a second for
  a label that changes once.

### Production shell

`src/app/Storage.ts` (settings + per-map records, defensive about corrupt
storage) and `src/ui/SettingsScreen.ts` (quality, shadows, FOV, look speed,
volume, frame counter, reset progress) wired through `Game.applySettings`.
Settings persist; the map picker shows "3 of 5 found" / "Cleared · 2:14"; the
results card gained a Best column and a "New best" tag; the last map is
remembered; the game pauses on blur or tab hide; `webglcontextlost` stops the
loop and shows a restoring card instead of a black screen and a console full of
errors.

### Measured

Interleaved A/B in one page (old config reconstructed at runtime, `tmp-ab.mjs`):
median frame 28.0 ms → 19.4 ms. Under SwiftShader the absolute numbers drift
±40% over a run, so the median of three interleaved rounds is the only figure
worth quoting; every new round beat the old round before it. The structural
wins — one shadow rebuild per 2.5+ frames instead of one per frame, 4.4× fewer
shadow texels, the composer skipped while idle, a 1.25 pixel cap instead of
2.0 — are the ones that matter on real hardware.

### Two layout bugs found on the way

The results card outgrew a 559 px viewport and its "Main menu" button was
unreachable (`.menu-card` now scrolls; `.results-card` pins its actions and
scrolls only the list). And the settings sheet showed the raw browser
scrollbar — styled to a 7 px ink trough like the map grid.
