## Photo Hunt — collision system overhaul

- Replaced the old system (14 hardcoded obstacle names + 2D AABBs with no Y test) with **real-triangle collision**: `CollisionGeometry` builds one compound collider per prop from actual mesh triangles. Player is a capsule r=0.30 h=1.75 step=0.35 skin=0.02, band-limited to `[ground+step, ground+height]` so tabletops block but awnings/pebbles don't.

Result: 149 parts / 309 meshes / 110,765 tris.

## Round 2 — play-test bug reports (jitter + shoreline / wooden structures)

Two reports: judder walking forward at the amphitheatre; unwalkable shoreline and walking through wooden structures. Four defects found, all proven by instrumenting the resolver in the running game:

1. **Step-over decided from the player's own ground.** The band was `[feet + stepHeight, feet + height]` with `feet` = terrain *under the player*, so on a slope a 0.34 m bench rail flipped between "step over" and "wall" every frame. Being pushed back uphill raised the feet 38 mm, which re-disabled the rail: a permanent 8 cm limit cycle. Fix: each triangle records the ground it stands on at build time (`CollisionGeometry.build(root, groundAt)`), and the test is `top > min(groundUnderTri, groundUnderFeet) + stepHeight`, plus "rises above the feet" (so a deck you stand on is not a wall) and "reaches below head height".
2. **Vertical triangles acted as infinite regions.** A wall end cap has three equal x values → degenerate in XZ → `pointInTriangle2D`'s sign tests collapse and round-off says "inside", so penetration came out as `radius + distance`. Measured 0.57 m penetration / 0.59 m ejection at the bench end cap; 0.03 / 0.05 after. This caused every 0.5 m teleport in the sweep.
3. **Point-sampled support lost plank decks.** Decks have 1–2 cm plank gaps; a single sample landed in a gap and returned the crossbeam 11 cm lower, leaving the deck top above the feet so the deck blocked the body standing on it. `supportHeight` now samples the footprint (centre + 4 at half radius).
4. **Resolution could reverse the step.** In a convex corner (chopping block at (1,3)) the nearest-facet normal alternates between facets, rotating the push and orbiting the body 1 cm with 73 reversals/120 frames. Resolution may cancel a step but never reverse it.

Verification: stress sweep 1,840 walks 17 → **2** oscillating (worst 0.527 m → 0.031 m); reported site **0 flips, stops at z=19.83**; amphitheatre all 8 headings **0 flips**; 526 valid forward walks **0** with ≥2 reversals; furniture 0/8 climbing, 53/53 touching, 0 penetrations; 39/39 tests (4 new, each checked against its fix reverted), tsc clean, build clean, production bundle verified at runtime (149 parts / 110,765 tris / 426 ms build).

**Measurement pitfalls worth remembering:** a test that passes with the fix reverted pins nothing — always revert and re-run. A synthetic box does *not* reproduce the degenerate-triangle round-off, so that one is evidenced in the real scene. Counting direction reversals along the walk axis misreads a *sideways slide* (the dome tent at the reported spot) as judder — check the actual contact normals before believing it. And a sweep that does not skip starts already inside geometry reports spawn-inside-the-van ejections as "jitter".

Remaining: 2/1,840 walks (diagonal onto the amphitheatre tiers, ≤3 cm) — that prop straddles the step threshold (seat ~0.28 m, back rest ~0.36 m), needing a per-part furniture-vs-platform decision rather than a bug fix. The boardwalk deck is not flat (first 0.5 m at -3.29, rest at -3.40) — kit geometry. MSI still NOT PRODUCED.

## Round 3 — jump animation (Space = jump, F = photo)

Added the user's `raw-anims/Jumping.fbx` as a playable jump and moved photo capture off Space.

**Pipeline:** `tools/convert-fbx-anim.mjs <fbx> <name>` converts Mixamo FBX to the compact JSON `CharacterAnimator` loads, writing to `downloaded_anims/` and `public/assets/anims/`. It patches a copy of three's FBXLoader (`tools/.cache/`, regenerated) because `parseAnimationLayers` throws on Mixamo's leftover T-pose curves and the loader uses relative imports.

**Three data traps, all now handled:**
1. The FBX has **two takes** — a stale 1 s "Walk" and the real 1.9 s "mixamo.com". `animations[0]` converts the wrong one; the script prefers `mixamo.com`, refuses when ambiguous, and accepts `--take=`.
2. Mixamo writes unanimated bones (`Face_Jaw`) as **one key** while body tracks have 58. On a shared time array three derives `valueSize = 4/58`, allocates a zero-length mixer buffer, and `PropertyMixer.saveOriginalState` loops forever on first `play()` — the tab freezes with no exception. Fix: resample every track onto the densest grid; plus `buildClip` now skips mismatched tracks with a warning. Diagnosed with `Debugger.pause` + `evaluateOnCallFrame` on the blocked frame.
3. Hip translation must be an offset from the rig's **bind pose** (94.99 cm, read from the file) in metres — matches the existing walk clip's convention exactly.

**Vertical design:** the clip's hip curve is split at build time — `rootLift` (airborne, 0→0.560 m) moves the player root so the camera follows; `hips` keeps only compression (0→−0.432 m) in the skeleton so the legs bend for a height the body is actually at. `hips + rootLift` reconstructs the source curve (unit-tested). The collision probe gets the **un-lifted** ground, so being airborne cannot let the body climb onto tables.

**Controller:** `PlayerController.update(..., jumpRequested)`; lift applied after `mixer.update`; `locomotion()` yields while jumping but takes over once the hips recover past −0.15 m and the player is moving (otherwise the landing pose slides). Idle now settles through `moveCapsule(zero)` so standing still on a boardwalk no longer drops to the terrain (was `getGroundHeight`, deck-blind) — 15/17 deck parts now hold, 1 has no plank under any sample point, 1 is a teleport-into-railing artifact.

**Verified:** dev + production bundle in headless Chrome — Space jumps (not captures), F captures, apex +0.560 m at 0.77 s, 34 airborne frames, lands exactly, second Space mid-air ignored, jump-again works, 10.3 m travelled while airborne, no console errors. 48 tests (9 new), tsc clean, build clean. Screenshots in `docs/jump-*.png`, write-up in `docs/JUMP_ANIMATION.md`.

**Pitfall:** a 1.9 s clip is longer than the airborne phase — my first behaviour test "failed" only because it stepped 1.5 s and expected the locomotion clip back. Check clip duration before calling a still-playing action a bug.

## Round 4 — five play-test bugs (map edge, lake, jump slide, jumping over things, docks)

All five came in one message, with the user offering to answer questions. Three answers set the design: walk on the lake bed (no water block), only low obstacles need to be jumpable (no standing on props), and the invisible wall goes exactly at the terrain edge (not the old ±48 square).

**Fixed earlier in the round:** `±48` clamp → `hasGround()` over the heightfield's NaN cells, checked per sub-step; water veto + `isWet()` deleted; `moveCapsule(..., bodyFeetY)` so the collision band follows the body (this is what made jumping over a bench work — the band was measured from the ground under the player); `getMoveScale()` returns 0 while the jump has the feet planted, killing the crouch/landing slide.

**The dock bug was one rule.** `triangleIsSolid` asked "low enough to step over?" of the ground **under the triangle**, and for a plank on the lake bed that is the bed, 0.5 m down. So every plank was a wall unless the feet were within 5 cm of it. The creek footbridge (a ramp 4 cm per 25 cm section, 1 m above the creek) was impassable from the first plank: **1.07 m with the rule disabled, 13.95 m with it.** Fix: `if (flat && top <= feetY + stepHeight) return false;` as the *first* test — anything the support query would put you on top of must not push you sideways. `flat` = the same `|normal.y| >= 0.7` predicate `supportHeight` uses, baked per triangle at build time (`triFlat`, 1 byte/tri).

**Stepping off a dock edge snapped 0.35 m in one frame** (the "teleport" on the bridges): `resolveVertical` glued any drop up to `stepHeight`. New `stepDown: 0.15` config; bigger drops fall under gravity. Max single-frame drop on the pier walk 0.343 → 0.165 m.

**Measurement notes:** walking a run along a module's *own* axis walks off the side — the modules are chained, so walk the chain direction. A state BFS (cell + body height) is the only honest reachability model: a per-cell height computed from the terrain can't represent standing on a deck. `walk()` in the collision tests now passes the body height, like the controller; without it depenetration measures from the ground under the deck and ejects the body backwards. And a coarse single-slab ramp does not reproduce the bug — one triangle's `top` spans the whole slope, so the test ramp must be tessellated like the real one.

**Verified** (dev + production bundle): footbridge 13.95 m both ends, beach→dock→end 20.24 m, dock/boardwalk deck cells 97–100 % standable, Space still lifts +0.55 m, W moves with real key events, F captures, no console errors. 52 tests (3 new: ramp, ledge drop, small step down), tsc clean, build clean. Write-up §11 in `docs/COLLISION_OVERHAUL.md`, screenshots `docs/{footbridge,dock,boardwalk}-crossing.png`.

**Left alone:** you still can't climb onto a dock from the water where the step exceeds `stepHeight` (raising it would make 0.4 m furniture climbable); a `life-jacket-post` sits in the second dock's entrance (layout); being inside a large footprint (walking off the pier into a moored canoe) still shoves 0.68 m in one frame — capping it needs the tunnelling safety net to accept an overlapping position, which risks freezing the body.

## Round 5 — jump feel + the landing that played in mid-air

Two reports after "oke ngon": the jump doesn't feel real (suspects the crouch takes too long), and jumping high→low leaves the feet standing in the air at the end of the clip. Both were measured, both were one cause each.

**Measured before:** take-off 0.500 s after the press, airtime 0.567 s, movement locked for 0.800 s after touchdown, whole jump 1.867 s. The clip is 1.90 s = 0.533 crouch + 0.600 flight + 0.767 recovery, so **1.3 s of it carries no travel**.

**Fix 1 — one playback rate per phase.** `applyJumpRate()` sets `action.timeScale` every frame from the action's own time: 3.0 while `t < jumpTakeoffTime`, **1.0 during the flight**, 2.4 after `jumpContactTime`. The flight must keep rate 1: the arc comes from `rootLift` sampled at clip time, so re-timing it makes the body hang or the feet skate. After: take-off 0.167 s, airtime 0.533 s, post-landing lock 0.333 s, whole jump 1.033 s. Boundaries are **read from the clip** (`readJumpPhases`): takeoff = first key with `rootLift > 0.01`; contact = first key after the flight back at 0; release = last key with hip offset below 35 % of the crouch depth.

**Fix 2 — the arc is anchored on the take-off ground.** `Math.max(takeoffY + rootLift, groundY)` can only ever come back down to where it started, so jumping off any drop finished the arc in the air and played the whole landing crouch at the take-off height. Now: when `isJumpArcFinished()` and the landing is still below, `holdJumpAtContact()` pins `action.time` to contact and sets `paused`, the controller integrates gravity, and `landJump()` releases the absorb on touchdown. `getMoveScale()` returns 1 while held (free fall ≠ planted feet). **Trap found by the harness:** on touchdown the arc must be re-anchored (`takeoffY = groundY`) — during the absorb `rootLift` is 0, which reads as "arc over, landing below" again, and the body snapped back up and re-fell forever.

**Fix 3 — the camera was riding the arc** at the horizontal damping rate (10/s), which is most of the weightlessness. New `camera.verticalDamping = 3.2` and `camera.jumpFollow = 0.25`: the vertical aim follows `PlayerController.getGroundY()` (the surface), plus a quarter of the gap.

**Verification method worth reusing:** stop the game loop (`h.game.loop.stop()`), then step `h.game.update(1/60)` by hand and read the **world Y of `LeftToeBase`/`RightToeBase`** (`bone.matrixWorld.elements[13]`). The toe bone sits ~0.07 m above the sole, so 0.07 = planted. Must call `updateMatrixWorld(true)` each step or the bone positions are one render stale (this produced nonsense on the first attempt). CDP screenshots take ~4 s each in swiftshader, so wall-clock screenshot sampling cannot catch a 1 s jump — deterministic stepping + one screenshot per phase is the only workable approach.

**Results:** flat — toes 0.073 at touchdown; 0.8 m step — held 11 frames, lands 0.078; 4.4 m cliff — held 33 frames, lands 0.078, absorb on the ground. Dev and production identical. 57 tests (5 new, each verified to fail against the old code), tsc + build clean.

## Round 6 — the roof that was its own wall, the hammock that was its own fence

**Report 1 — "jumped from the mountain onto a roof, the foot touched and the body teleported".** Not the support query (that was round 5). `triangleIsSolid` reads `triBounds[o+3]`, the triangle's *highest* corner. The wash house roof is one long sheet from eave 1.5 to ridge 2.69, so a body standing at 2.24 near the eave was below that corner and every test in the function called the sheet a wall; `circleTriangle2D` then reported a 1.11 m penetration and pushed it out sideways (measured: x 2.00 → 3.31 → 4.33 on consecutive frames). Fix: measure a *flat* triangle from its **lowest** corner — `if (flat && tb[o + 2] <= feetY + stepHeight) return false;` — so a floor underfoot or within a step never blocks, while a deck too high to step onto still does. After: drop onto the roof settles at 2.37 with max frame step 0.00; walking down the roof keeps x at 2.00 and steps off the eave into a 2.3 m fall. **Re-measured the whole deck corpus with and without the rule: identical within 0.02 m** (18 piers/bridges/ramps), so the change costs nothing on decks.

**Report 2 — "can stand on the hammock but cannot walk off it".** Round 5's fix put `/hammock/i` in `NON_SOLID_PATTERNS`, which drops the mesh from the geometry entirely — the cloth lost its *floor* too, so the only standable thing left was the two post caps at 1.0 m. A hammock must be a floor and not a wall, so it needs its own list: `NEVER_WALL_PATTERNS = [/hammock/i]`, a `neverWall` flag on the part, and `continue` in `collectContacts`/`deepestContact`. Cloth sags to 0.10 m at the centre; dropped from 1.8 m the body settles at 0.10 and walks off in all four directions with zero blocking frames.

**Report 3 — "walking uphill is faster".** Horizontal speed constant ⇒ surface speed grows as 1/cos. Measured 0.080 across + 0.122 up per frame (surface 0.146) against 0.080 flat. Fix: scale by cos(slope), floor 0.55, and **sample the ground 0.3 m ahead** via a new `WorldCollision.groundAt(x, z, feetY)` (terrain or support, same query the move uses). The first version used the previous frame's own step — exact but one frame late, which is a 0.146 m lunge at the foot of every hill. Disabled while airborne: a bench edge passing under the feet reads as a 1.4 gradient and brakes the body for a frame. After: uphill 0.044 / 0.067 = 0.080 surface.

**Report 4 — "one frame jerks while falling".** The clip's last three root-lift keys decelerate (a landing cushion). Jumping off a hill those keys play 3 m up, so the fall dipped to 40 % for two frames: 0.0597 → 0.0236 → 0.0236 → 0.0492. Fix in the animator: `readJumpPhases` records `jumpImpactTime`/`jumpPeakFallSpeed` (steepest descent, 0.05 s window); `resolveVertical` takes over the vertical as soon as `isArcCushioning()` and `groundY < takeoffY - 0.12`, seeding `fallSpeed` with the peak. After: 0.0597 → 0.0602 → 0.0652 → 0.0702, monotone. A jump landing on its own ground is untouched.

**Known open:** walking off the back of the amphitheatre bench ejects the body 0.36 m in one frame (`depenetrate` when the centre is inside a large footprint) — documented, not fixed; capping it risks freezing the body.

**Harness note:** for screenshots the loop must keep running — `loop.stop()` also stops the renderer and every capture comes out empty. Place the body, set `cameraController.targetYaw/targetPitch/targetDistance`, sleep 1.4 s, capture. `?debug=1` is required for `__hunt`, and it also turns on the collision wireframe: call `game.collisionDebug.setEnabled(false)`.

## Round 8 — the kit map pipeline, eight maps, and the ground that was a wall

**Pipeline.** `tools/prepare-maps.mjs` turns each `maps/*.zip` into a playable map: extract `scene.glb`, bounding-box report (`tools/map-report.mjs`, pure Node, no three.js), Draco compress to `public/assets/maps/<id>.glb`, sidecar to `maps/reports/<id>.json`, regenerate `src/data/mapRegistry.ts`. `?map=<id>` selects. `--refresh` recomputes the derived data from an already-extracted source without paying for the Draco pass again — that is what to run after changing the spawn heuristic. 356 MB of archives → 27 MB of payload, 13.2× on average.

**Two bugs, both fatal, both the same shape.** (1) Terrain detection tested `mesh.name.includes('terrain')`; every kit names the *node* (`<map>-terrain`) and leaves the mesh unnamed, so nothing was registered and every map was walked on a flat y = 0 plane. (2) That same terrain node is a child of the placement group, so it also became a collision part — and its triangles span the map from a −12 m skirt to the hilltops, which made the ground itself a wall. The construction site was blocked 119/120 frames in all four directions. Fixes: `findTerrainMeshes` matches node *or* mesh name and collects every descendant mesh (construction site has nine `ground` slabs beside its terrain sheet); the heightfield rasterises all of them keeping the highest surface per cell; `build()` skips `-terrain$` parts and takes the terrain meshes as an explicit skip set.

**Spawn is a hint, not a promise.** Bounding boxes cannot know whether a point is walkable. Three maps each taught one lesson: construction-site (the lowest surface was a 3.2 m excavation floor), skatepark (the floor is a deck of `skate-slab-tile` pieces over a bowl — measured against the terrain every slab was a wall and the nearest walkable ground was 21 m away), dia-de-muertos (a terraced hillside; the union centre of the terrace pieces is on the slope *between* terraces). Offline: use the **mode** of object bottoms, not the minimum, and snap to the nearest floor-piece centre. Runtime: `WorldCollision.findSpawn(hint)` settles like a body (start at the hint's height, let `supportHeight` find the surface under it, drop by 0.6 m while the band is empty), requires the floor to vary by < 0.4 m within a body radius, and probes the resolver with a body 0.45 m fatter — "no overlap" then means air on every side. Rings widen from the hint.

**`?debug=1` no longer turns on the wireframe.** 1.2 M line vertices on a 400k-triangle map cost 300–3300 ms per frame under SwiftShader, and the character load queued behind those frames took 48 s. The overlay is `?wire=1` (or F3), built lazily. Load timings are now logged once per map (`[load] <id> total=… world=… debug=… player=… spawn=…`) plus `[collision]` and `worldLoader.timings` / `player.timings` on `__hunt`.
